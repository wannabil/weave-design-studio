04 / PROCESSCLARITY AT EVERY STAGE

A clear process, without forcing the answer.

Each commission is different, but the thinking follows a disciplined sequence. This keeps decisions visible and gives the design a reason to exist.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We listen, visit or study the site where appropriate, and understand the people, ambitions, constraints, budget and programme behind the project.

  2. 02

    Define

    We organise the brief, identify priorities and agree on the scope and key questions the design must answer.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Ideas are tested through drawings, spatial studies, material thinking and ongoing conversations—not presented as a single unexplained solution.

  4. 04

    Coordinate

    We help align the design with the relevant consultants, appointed professionals and next-stage requirements appropriate to the project.
